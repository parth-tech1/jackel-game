# 🕵️‍♂️ James Bond 007

**Status:** Active 
**Affiliation:** MI6 (Secret Intelligence Service) 
**Code Name:** 007 
**Designation:** Double-O Section (Licensed to Kill)

---

## 📄 Overview

**James Bond**, created by author **Ian Fleming** in 1953, is a British Secret Service agent working for **MI6**. Operating under the codename **007**, Bond is a commander in the Royal Naval Reserve and a deadly operative known for his sophistication, resourcefulness, and ability to thwart global threats. He is typically depicted as a dashing, martini-drinking, gadget-reliant spy with a complicated personal life and an unmatched record of saving the world.

---

## 🎬 Key Characters & Allies

*   **M**: The Head of MI6 and Bond's direct superior.
*   **Q**: The head of Q Branch (Research and Development), responsible for providing Bond with high-tech gadgets, armor-clad cars, and weaponized everyday items.
*   **Miss Moneypenny**: M's personal secretary, who shares a famous, flirtatious banter with Bond.
*   **Felix Leiter**: A CIA operative and Bond’s closest ally and friend from the American intelligence community.

---

## 👥 The Actors of 007

The cinematic legacy of Bond spans six decades, portrayed by six official actors:

1.  **Sean Connery** (1962–1967, 1971, 1983) - *The original, setting the standard for charm and grit.*
2.  **George Lazenby** (1969) - *The emotional, one-time Bond.*
3.  **Roger Moore** (1973–1985) - *The witty, campy, and longest-serving Bond (by film count).*
4.  **Timothy Dalton** (1987–1989) - *The dark, intense, and book-accurate Bond.*
5.  **Pierce Brosnan** (1995–2002) - *The sleek, action-packed 90s Bond.*
6.  **Daniel Craig** (2006–2021) - *The gritty, grounded, and modern era of Bond.*

## 🔫 Trademark Elements

*   **The Gun barrel Sequence:** The opening cinematic visual of Bond seen through the bore of a gun, which he turns and shoots.
*   **"Shaken, not stirred":** His legendary vodka martini preference.
*   **The Cars:** The iconic **Aston Martin DB5**, complete with ejector seats and machine guns.
*   **The Gadgets:** Laser watches, explosive pens, and tracking devices.
*   **The Nemesis:** **SPECTRE** (Special Executive for Counter-intelligence, Terrorism, Revenge and Extortion), led by the villainous **Ernst Stavro Blofeld**.
